Where kitchen leaks start in desert homes

Most kitchen water comes from a short list: braided faucet lines, drain slip nuts, the disposal housing, and the line to an ice maker or filter. A slow drip can run for weeks while the cabinet floor soaks it up.

Cold causes fewer leaks here than people expect, but it does happen. NOAA 1991 to 2020 normals count about 16.6 nights a year when the low reaches 32 F or colder, mostly in December and January. If your sink sits on an outside wall, open the cabinet doors on those nights so room air reaches the pipes.

What you see under the sink and what it means

Photograph anything you find before you wipe it.

  • A swollen or crumbling cabinet floor means water has sat there a while.
  • A lifted toe kick or darkened baseboard means water traveled along the floor.
  • Raised or gapped plank edges mean moisture is underneath.
  • A musty smell with no puddle often means a wet cavity behind a cabinet or dishwasher panel.

Kitchen safety in the first hour

Close the small angle stop valve under the sink. If that fails, shut the main valve to the house. Stay out of standing water that reaches outlets or the dishwasher connection, and cut power only if the panel can be reached dry.

A gas smell near the range means leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first. Sparking gets the same response. Then call dispatch and say what is wet.

How crews dry a kitchen without tearing it all out

A crew begins with a meter sweep and an infrared camera pass, because water often travels farther than the puddle suggests. Toe kicks come off and a few panels may open. After that, LGR dehumidifiers and air movers work on the cavity and subfloor together.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published guide that sorts wet jobs by how dirty the water was, so ask a crew if it works to it. Cabinets come out only when drying in place will not work. After that, the cabinet, counter and flooring rebuild begins.

Renters, landlords and who pays

Census Bureau QuickFacts puts the owner-occupied housing rate in Twentynine Palms at 38.2 percent for 2020 to 2024, so most households rent. The person who finds the leak is often not the one who owns the cabinets. A tenant should report it to the landlord in writing and take photos.

Who pays depends on the lease, the cause and both policies. A renter's policy generally covers the tenant's belongings and a landlord's policy generally covers the building, so ask each insurer.

Kitchen mistakes that make it worse

Do not caulk over a wet cabinet floor or lay new vinyl on it, since that traps moisture. Skip the heat gun on a swollen counter edge, which can warp laminate.

What sets the scope of a kitchen job

Two kitchens with the same leak can need very different work.

Cabinet material

Plywood boxes dry far more often than particleboard, which tends to swell for good.

How long it leaked

A one-day spill and a six-week drip are different jobs, even under the same sink.

Floor layers

Vinyl over underlayment over subfloor holds water in ways bare tile does not.

Countertop type

A loose slab of stone lifts away easily, while a glued laminate top is hard to separate.

Spread to other rooms

Water that crossed into a hallway or dining area adds drying space and equipment.

A general note on insurance

The California Department of Insurance describes sudden and accidental water damage as generally covered and damage from seepage or leaks as generally not. A supply line that bursts overnight and a drain that dripped for months are treated very differently. Check your policy and ask your insurer.

Frequently asked questions

Is emergency kitchen water damage restoration available in Twentynine Palms?

Yes. Someone answers the dispatch line around the clock, weekends and holidays too. A crew goes out the same day whenever one is free, though no arrival time is ever promised. Close the angle stop under the sink first if you can reach it.

Can my particleboard cabinets be dried, or do they have to go?

Particleboard and MDF swell when soaked and rarely shrink back. Light exposure may be dried, but a saturated cabinet floor is usually replaced. Plywood and solid wood have better odds.

How do I tell if water got under my kitchen floor?

Look for raised plank edges, a soft spot near the sink or a musty smell. A moisture meter and thermal camera can confirm it without lifting the floor.

I rent my home and the sink is leaking. Who do I call first?

Tell your landlord in writing and photograph the damage. If water is spreading, shut the valve and call us so drying can start.

Does a slow drip under the sink count as sudden damage?

Usually not. The state insurance department lists seepage as generally not covered, with sudden and accidental damage often covered. Your policy wording decides.
