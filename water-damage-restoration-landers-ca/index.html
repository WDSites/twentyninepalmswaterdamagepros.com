Cold-season nights: pipes, pressure tanks and well houses

Spring start-up: cooling systems and idle plumbing

Equipment that sat idle all winter shows its weak spots in the first week of use. Check evaporative cooler lines, air conditioner condensate drains and water heater connections while you are home.

A slow drip that runs for weeks is often treated differently by insurers than a sudden break. If a ceiling has stained or a wall feels soft, drying and then water damage repair of the drywall is the usual path.

Mold needs moisture to start, so ask any company whether its mold work follows ANSI/IICRC S520. Health questions belong with a physician.
