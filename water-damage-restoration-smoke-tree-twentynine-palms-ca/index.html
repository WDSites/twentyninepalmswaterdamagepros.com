Hot summers put cooling equipment to work

NOAA climate normals for Twentynine Palms show a July average high of about 104 degrees and roughly 89 days a year above 100 degrees. The July average low is above 77 degrees, so cooling equipment runs through the night. Hours of running add up to a lot of condensation, and a clogged condensate line or a failing evaporative cooler can drip into a ceiling or wall for days.

A stain that grows after a hot day, a musty smell near the air handler or a wet ceiling tile below an attic unit are all signs to check. Our page on AC leak water damage explains how those slow leaks are found and dried.

Cold nights and the occasional frozen pipe

The same NOAA data shows about 17 days a year when the low is at or below freezing, mostly in December and January. Freezes here are occasional, not constant, which is exactly why exposed pipes and outdoor spigots get missed. A pipe that freezes and splits may show nothing until it thaws.

If you find a frozen pipe, close the main valve if it is dry and within reach, open a nearby faucet and call before it thaws. Frozen pipe water damage covers what happens once a split pipe starts to run.

Water heaters and supply lines

Water heaters and supply lines are the other common sources in an ordinary house. A tank that weeps at the base, a toilet line that sweats and then drips and a dishwasher hose that loosens all release clean water at first. That is the easiest water to dry, but only if it is caught before it soaks insulation and the subfloor.

See water heater leak cleanup if the leak starts at the tank. Do not assume a small puddle means a small problem, because water travels under flooring and along framing.

Safety steps before you touch anything

  • Close the main water valve if it is dry and within easy reach. If you must step through water to reach it, stop and call.
  • Switch off the water heater at its own shutoff if the leak is at the tank, and keep away from the electrical connection.
  • Keep power off to wet outlets and ceiling fixtures. Never touch a switch while you stand on wet floor.
  • Move furniture and rugs out of the wet zone only if you can do it without walking through standing water.
  • Take photographs and a short video of the damage before cleanup starts.

What drying looks like in a house with a slow leak

A slow leak is mapped before it is dried. A crew uses moisture meters and a thermal camera to follow the wet area through walls and under floors, and sometimes opens a small section to confirm. Then air movers and dehumidifiers dry the materials that can be saved while wet insulation or pad is removed.

The industry reference for the process is ANSI/IICRC S500. Readings are repeated until the structure reaches its dry target, which is how you know drying is finished rather than guessing by feel. Our page on structural drying describes the equipment in detail.

Insurance and the line between sudden and gradual

According to the California Department of Insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, but slow seepage, repeated leaks and wear and tear usually are not. A burst pipe and a slow drip can look similar on the ceiling, so the cause matters a great deal to a claim.

That is why records help. Photograph the damage, keep receipts, and note when you first saw it. Coverage depends entirely on your policy, and nothing on this page promises that any claim will be paid.

Small habits that reduce the odds

  • Have the condensate line and drain pan checked before the hot season.
  • Insulate exposed pipes and outdoor spigots before the first cold snap.
  • Replace rubber washing machine and dishwasher hoses on a schedule.
  • Learn where the main shutoff valve is before you need it.

None of these can guarantee a leak will not happen. For repairs after drying, see water damage repair.

Smoke Tree water damage questions

Where is Smoke Tree in Twentynine Palms?

The federal names database places Smoke Tree southwest of the center of Twentynine Palms by about a mile and a half. No official boundary was found, so call and describe your street if you are unsure.

How often does it freeze in Twentynine Palms?

NOAA climate normals show about 17 days a year when the low reaches freezing, mostly in December and January. Freezes are occasional, so exposed pipes and spigots are the most likely to be caught off guard.

Why does my AC cause water damage?

Cooling equipment removes moisture from the air and drains it away. If the line clogs or a pan overflows, the water can run into a ceiling or wall slowly, often for days before a stain shows.

Is a slow leak covered by insurance?

Slow seepage and repeated leaks usually fall outside homeowners coverage, according to the state insurance department, while sudden and accidental damage is often covered. Your policy and the cause decide it, so ask your insurer.

How do I know the structure is actually dry?

Moisture readings are taken against a dry target and repeated until the materials meet it. A surface that feels dry can still be wet behind it.
