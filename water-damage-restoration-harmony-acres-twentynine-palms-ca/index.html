Why septic systems change the water damage picture

The county boundary commission's 2022 review of the Twentynine Palms community found no organized sewer collection system in the city. Nearly every home and business drains to an individual septic system, with the Marine base housing area the main exception. In December 2023 the city council voted to return a 50 million dollar state grant for a sewer system, and the project was reported on hold at that time.

No source says Harmony Acres has more sewage trouble than other parts of town. The point is simpler. When nearly every house relies on a septic tank, a backup into a bathtub or floor drain is a common worry, and the water involved is not clean.

Toilet overflows, slow drains and backups

A septic backup usually shows up at the lowest drain in the house. Warning signs include gurgling pipes, several fixtures draining slowly at once, sewage smell in the yard and a toilet that rises when another fixture drains. If water is already on the floor, it should be treated as Category 3, meaning grossly contaminated.

The cause of a backup sits with the tank, the pipe or the leach field, and a septic contractor handles that part. Cleanup of the contaminated floors, walls and belongings is a separate job. Our page on sewage backup cleanup explains how the two jobs fit together.

Safety steps when the water may contain sewage

  • Stay out of the water and keep children and pets away. The EPA advises avoiding contact with water that may contain raw sewage.
  • Stop using sinks, toilets, showers and the washing machine so more wastewater does not enter the house.
  • Do not use a household vacuum, mop or fan on sewage water. They spread contamination and can fail in standing water.
  • Close the water supply only if the valve is dry and easy to reach, and leave electrical panels alone if the floor is wet.
  • Photograph the damage from the doorway before anything is moved.

The CDC adds that soaked porous items such as rugs and mattresses that touched contaminated water should be thrown away, and that bleach should never be mixed with other cleaners.

How contaminated water is cleaned differently

Clean water from a supply line is dried in place. Sewage water is different, because carpet, pad, insulation and the lower part of drywall that soaked it are usually removed instead of dried. Hard surfaces are cleaned with a disinfectant and then dried, and the area is often sealed off with plastic barriers so nothing spreads to dry rooms.

Crews use extraction units, air movers, dehumidifiers and HEPA air scrubbers, and they check moisture readings until the structure is dry. The industry reference is ANSI/IICRC S500, which classifies water by contamination level. It describes the method and does not by itself certify any company.

Other leaks inside the home

Not every call in a septic neighborhood is sewage. Washing machine hoses, water heaters and kitchen supply lines fail here as they do anywhere. The cleaner the water, the more can usually be saved, so say what the water looks like and smells like when you call. Appliance leak water damage covers those cases.

Insurance questions after a backup

Coverage for sewer or septic backup varies by policy, and many policies treat it separately from sudden plumbing leaks. Ask your insurer exactly what your policy says before assuming either way. This page cannot tell you whether a claim will be paid.

Photos, an inventory of what was lost and drying logs all help build the record. The state insurance department also tells policyholders to keep cleanup receipts and wait for the adjuster before making lasting repairs.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Pouring bleach or drain chemicals into a backed up system.
  • Pumping the tank and assuming the floors are fine. The tank and the soaked structure are two separate problems.
  • Keeping carpet that sat in sewage water to save money.
  • Waiting several days before getting the structure dried, because wet materials can start to grow mold.

Where is Harmony Acres in Twentynine Palms?

Do homes in Harmony Acres use septic tanks?

A 2022 county review found the city has no organized sewer system except for the Marine base housing area, so most homes use septic. It does not list Harmony Acres by name, so confirm your own property with the city or your records.

Who fixes a septic backup, and who cleans up after it?

A septic contractor finds and repairs the tank, pipe or leach field problem. A restoration professional cleans and dries the floors and walls that the water reached. They are usually two different jobs.

Can I clean up a toilet overflow myself?

A small, contained overflow of clean water may be manageable. Anything that contains sewage should be left to professionals, because it carries germs and soaks into porous materials.

Will insurance pay for sewer backup damage?

It depends on your policy, since sewer backup is often a separate coverage. Ask your insurer before you assume a claim will be paid.
